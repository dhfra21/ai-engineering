How the guide maps to your answer:
- grade "good": the explanation passes all four criteria in §2.
- grade "bad": it fails at least one. List every failed criterion in failed_criteria.
- grade "drop": the item is broken as data (§4: the SQL is ambiguous, the author's note contradicts the SQL, or the explanation is empty or garbled). Not for hard calls (§3 rule 9).
- reason: 1-3 sentences naming the specific problem, or why it passes. This is your note on the label.
- failed_criteria must be empty unless grade is "bad".
- Check every claim against the SQL and the result rows. The SQL is the ground truth.
- Answer in JSON with the fields reason, failed_criteria, grade.
